# Aircraft maintenance: hangar space is the constraint

Airlines are flying older aircraft for longer, which keeps demand for heavy maintenance high across Europe. Independent maintenance providers with hangar space are booked well into 2028, and most are paying for parts in US dollars while earning in sterling or euros.
