# Grocery: regional chains grow stores as margins tighten

Regional grocers in the Northeast are adding stores while gross margins tighten under higher labor and freight costs. Supplier finance programs are becoming a common way to keep produce suppliers on good terms without stretching the grocer's own balance sheet.

Harbor & Pine is among the better-capitalized chains, with net leverage around 1.6x EBITDA and a growing private-label range.
