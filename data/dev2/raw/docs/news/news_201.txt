Headline: Harbor & Pine to open 12 stores in upstate New York
Client-Ref: C201

PORTLAND, Maine - Harbor & Pine Foods will open 12 stores in upstate New York over the next 18 months, the regional grocer said on Tuesday, its first expansion outside New England. The first two stores, in Albany and Saratoga Springs, are due to open in March 2027.
