# Plant visit - Vossberg, Stuttgart

Attendees: Matthias Keller (CEO), Katrin Brandt (CFO), Jonas Weber (Head of Treasury); Sofia Marchetti, Henrik Lund (bank).

## Discussion
- Vossberg is building a plant in Queretaro, Mexico, to supply its US customers locally; production starts in the second quarter of 2027.
- About 60% of sales go to US carmakers and are paid in dollars, while most costs are in euros. Jonas wants to hedge 12 months of dollar receipts.
- Katrin said Vossberg has committed to our receivables purchase facility for EUR 40 million (about $43M) of US customer invoices, starting in January.
- Matthias asked for an introduction to the bank's Mexico team.

## Actions
- Henrik to send the EUR/USD hedging proposal.
