Headline: Vossberg to build EUR 120 million plant in Mexico
Client-Ref: C301

STUTTGART, Oct 27 (Reuters) - German drivetrain maker Vossberg Antriebstechnik will build a EUR 120 million plant in Queretaro, Mexico, to supply US carmakers, Chief Executive Matthias Keller said on Tuesday.
