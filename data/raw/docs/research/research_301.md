# German auto suppliers move production closer to US customers

German suppliers are adding capacity in Mexico to serve US carmakers without long shipping times. Most still report in euros, so dollar revenue from Mexican plants raises their FX exposure.

Vossberg, a mid-sized family-owned drivetrain maker, is one of several suppliers making the move.
